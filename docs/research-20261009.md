# Is mining worth it on Astra's PC? (research, 2026-10-09)

Prepared by Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`). **These are first-pass estimates from public articles, not measurements.** Prices and difficulty change daily. Recheck before deciding.

## Short answer
**Probably close to break-even, and only if the PC is already on anyway.** At Colorado home electricity prices, mining on a PC you already own makes somewhere between a small loss and well under a dollar a day. Buying hardware to mine would lose money. Mining is not a way to make real money here. It could still make sense as a hobby the mole keeps an eye on.

## Astra's hardware (read from Windows)
| Part | What | Fit for mining |
|---|---|---|
| CPU | AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D, 8 cores | Good for **Monero (XMR)**. Its RandomX algorithm is built for CPUs, and the big X3D cache helps. |
| GPU | AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT | Poor fit. Monero is CPU-first, and Bitcoin needs ASIC machines. GPU coins pay little. Not researched in depth. |

## The best candidate: Monero on the CPU with XMRig
- **Why Monero:** CPU mining still makes sense there. Since 2022 every block pays a fixed 0.6 XMR (the "tail emission"), so rewards don't halve away.
- **Why not Bitcoin:** It is mined by ASIC machines. A home PC earns effectively nothing.
- **The tool:** XMRig is the standard open-source miner, mining to a pool such as P2Pool.

## Rough numbers (estimates, not measured)
| | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 7800X3D hashrate | roughly 13–16 kH/s | scaled from published RandomX figures. **Measure it.** |
| Coins per day | about 0.002 XMR | a 7950X at ~27 kH/s is quoted at 0.003–0.004 XMR/day |
| Value per day | about $0.40–$0.80 | XMR quoted between ~$200 and ~$390 in 2026 articles, so they disagree |
| Power price | about $0.14–$0.16/kWh all-in | Colorado average (EIA via Voltflow; EnergySage). Xcel's base rate is lower, but riders add to it. |
| Extra power if the PC is on anyway | ~70–90 W, so ~2 kWh/day, about **$0.30/day** | CPU under full load minus idle |
| Power if the PC is on only to mine | ~150–200 W, so ~4 kWh/day, about **$0.55–$0.75/day** | whole system |

**So:** PC already on: roughly +$0.10 to +$0.50 a day. PC on only to mine: roughly −$0.35 to +$0.25 a day. Articles put break-even near $0.08–$0.10/kWh, below Colorado's rate.

## Costs that aren't money
- Heat and fan noise in the room, and it slows games, VR and Unity while it runs.
- Taxes: in the US, mined coins count as income at their value on the day you receive them. Finance Finch would need to know.
- Safety: miners are a favourite disguise for malware. Download XMRig only from its official GitHub releases and check the signature. Antivirus may flag it anyway.

## What to measure before deciding
1. Your real all-in price per kWh from an Xcel bill (total ÷ kWh).
2. A 10-minute XMRig benchmark (`xmrig --bench=1M`): actual hashrate and CPU package power.
3. Today's XMR price and a calculator such as the one on miningreturns.com.

## Options for the app
1. **Calculator only** (v0, built): enter hashrate, watts, price and power cost, and the mole says whether it's worth it.
2. **Watcher:** Astra runs XMRig herself, and the app reads XMRig's local HTTP API (read-only) for hashrate, temperature and earnings. The mole worries when it runs hot or at a loss.
3. **Controller:** a Windows app that starts and stops XMRig on a schedule (for example only when idle). This needs Astra's go-ahead first. See AGENTS.md.
